Commercial Identity

Surface: Codex Labs Commercial Surface v2.0

Operator: Kipp M. Wiggins — Founder, Codex Labs

The Codex Labs Store is the governed commercial layer of the Codex Labs OS. All products, pricing, and purchase protocols are authorized by the Founder and maintained with full continuity and deterministic guarantees.

Product Governance

  • All products are reviewed and authorized by the Founder before listing.
  • Product descriptions are accurate, complete, and governed — no misleading claims.
  • Product updates are versioned and logged; prior versions remain accessible in the product record.
  • No product is delisted without a documented governance action and customer notification where required.

Deterministic Pricing

  • All prices are listed clearly with no hidden fees or conditions.
  • Price changes require a governance action and are logged with effective date.
  • Prices in effect at time of purchase are honored — retroactive price changes do not apply to completed transactions.
  • Refund and exchange policies are documented and applied deterministically.

Continuity

The Commercial Surface maintains unbroken continuity across all product versions and transactions. All purchase records are immutable once confirmed. Product availability and terms are maintained with full traceability.

Purchase Protocols

  • Protocol P-01: All purchases are confirmed with a logged transaction record delivered to the buyer.
  • Protocol P-02: Digital products are delivered immediately upon payment confirmation.
  • Protocol P-03: Access credentials and delivery links are unique to each transaction and non-transferable unless explicitly permitted.
  • Protocol P-04: Disputes are handled through the Codex Labs governance layer — contact the Founder directly for resolution.
  • Protocol P-05: All commercial transactions are subject to the Codex Labs Commercial Surface terms, which are immutable at the time of transaction.
