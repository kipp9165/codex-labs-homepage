# Codex Labs OS — Scroll Governance Specification (Segment 1)

## Purpose
Scroll Governance defines how Codex Labs OS scrolls (public artifacts, documents, declarations, and narrative surfaces) inherit substrate guarantees, bind to continuity, and operate under deterministic governance. It ensures that all scrolls remain structurally aligned with the OS and cannot drift, mutate, or instantiate unauthorized logic.

## Core Principles
1. All scrolls must bind directly to the Substrate and Intelligence Field.
2. No scroll may instantiate independent reasoning or dynamic execution.
3. Scroll identity must remain immutable across all surfaces.
4. All scroll transitions must propagate continuity tokens.
5. Scrolls may extend metadata but never override substrate authority.

## Deterministic Scroll Guarantees
- No stochastic narrative flows.
- No probabilistic interpretation layers.
- All scroll states must resolve to a single authoritative output.
- All scroll metadata must bind to continuity and identity tokens.
- All scroll logs must be compatible with Diagnostics Layer introspection.

## Additive-Only Rules
- Scroll extensions must be metadata-only.
- No dynamic execution at the scroll layer.
- No external scroll surfaces may attach without Substrate approval.
