# DM-Only Sales Protocol

## Overview

Codex Labs uses a DM-only sales protocol. All customer conversations happen in direct messages (Twitter DM, LinkedIn DM, Slack DM, email, etc.). No sales calls. No sales meetings. No sales teams.

This document outlines how the sales process works.

---

## Why DM-Only?

1. **Founder preference.** Most founders hate sales calls. They'd rather DM.
2. **Asynchronous alignment.** DMs are async. Calls are sync. We do async.
3. **Written record.** DMs create a record. Easy to reference. No misunderstandings.
4. **Scalability.** One person can handle many conversations at once via DM.
5. **Respect.** We meet people where they are, not where we want them.

## DM Sales Workflow

### Stage 1: Cold DM (You Reach Out)

**Typical cold DM:**

*"Hi — I'm [name], founder/operator at [company]. We're dealing with [problem]. Saw your work on [reference]. Worth a conversation? What's the best way to learn more?"*

**What happens:**

- Codex Labs receives DM
- Response within 24–48 hours
- Response explains what we do + asks clarifying question
- You respond with more detail
- Conversation continues or doesn't, based on fit

**Keep in mind:**

- We get ~50 DMs/week
- We respond to all, but response time varies
- If it's not a fit, we'll say so politely

---

### Stage 2: Initial Qualification (Back and Forth)

**Questions you'll be asked:**

- What's your current state? (size, stage, revenue, team)
- What's the specific problem? (decision, direction, structure)
- What's your timeline? (urgent? 3 months?)
- What's your budget? (approx range, ballpark)
- Have you worked with consultants before? (context)

**Keep in mind:**

- Be specific. Vague DMs get slower responses.
- One topic per DM. Multiple questions slow things down.
- Share context upfront. Don't make us ask 5 times for details.

---

### Stage 3: Container Recommendation

**When it's a fit, you'll receive:**

- Container recommendation (Clarity Sprint, Deep Dive, Ongoing Advisory)
- Price range
- Timeline
- What to expect
- Next steps

**Format:** Usually a long, detailed DM or a link to intake.html

---

### Stage 4: Intake (If You're Ready)

**If interested:**

1. Go to /intake.html
2. Complete intake form
3. Submit
4. Codex Labs reviews within 24–48 hours
5. You receive email with next steps + Stripe invoice

**No sales call required.**

---

### Stage 5: Payment & Start

1. You pay Stripe invoice
2. Engagement begins
3. Shared doc is set up
4. Consultation starts

## Important Rules

### What We Won't Do

- **No sales calls.** Ever. Not even after intake.
- **No sales pitches.** We explain what we do once. That's it.
- **No follow-up sequences.** We don't spam DMs or emails.
- **No pressure.** If you're not sure, don't do it.
- **No calendar links.** "Book a time" doesn't happen here.

### What We Will Do

- **Answer your questions via DM.** Clearly. Within 24–48 hours.
- **Be direct about fit.** "This isn't for us" if it's not.
- **Explain the process clearly.** Intake → review → payment → start.
- **Make it easy to move forward.** One link. One form. Done.

## DM Best Practices

### For Cold Outreach

**Good DM:**

*"Hi — I'm Sarah, founder at TechCo. We scaled from 5 to 15 people in 6 months and organizational chaos has set in. Saw your work on operating systems. We're specifically stuck on: (1) ownership clarity, (2) decision authority. Wondering if this is your wheelhouse? Timeline: Want clarity in 3 weeks. Budget: ~$3K available. Open to learning more."*

**Bad DM:**

*"Hey, can we chat about your services?"*

---

### For Follow-Ups

**Good:**

*"Hey — following up on my DM from [date]. Curious about next steps or if this isn't the right fit."*

**Bad:**

*"You didn't respond, so I'm booking a call" OR "Can you call me?"*

---

### For Multiple Topics

**Good:** (Send as separate DMs)

*"One more question: What's your process for team input during the consulting engagement?"*

**Bad:** (All in one)

*"Also, what's your process? Also, can you do a call? Also, what if we need something custom?"*

---

## After You Decide to Work Together

**Nothing changes.** Communication stays in DM until intake is complete. After payment, you move to the shared doc/email channel for the actual consultation.

---

## Support After Engagement

**DM-only applies to sales. After you're a client, communication moves to:**
- Shared doc (primary)
- Email (secondary)
- DM for urgent escalations (emergency response, ~4 hours)

---

## Escalation Path

**If something goes wrong during an engagement:**

1. Send message in shared doc
2. If no response in 24 hours, email support
3. If critical emergency, DM for escalation

## Why This Approach Works

- **Respects your time.** No meetings. No calendar blocking.
- **Asynchronous compatibility.** DMs work whenever you want to send them.
- **Clear record.** You can reference the conversation anytime.
- **Self-selected fit.** People who engage via DM are already async-ready.
- **Faster decisions.** Written clarity forces good decision-making.

## Common Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Can I schedule a call to learn more?**
A: No. Send your questions via DM. We'll answer clearly.

**Q: What if I need to talk to a salesperson?**
A: You're talking to one. Via DM. That's the whole process.

**Q: How long does DM back-and-forth usually take?**
A: 2–5 DMs spread over 2–3 days (24–48 hr response time).

**Q: What if I'm not sure if this is right for me?**
A: Ask your questions via DM. We'll help you figure out fit. No pressure either way.

**Q: Can I get a custom proposal?**
A: Maybe. But we'll discuss via DM first. No sales deck. No fancy formatting.
