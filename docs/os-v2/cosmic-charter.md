# Codex Labs OS v2.0 — Cosmic Charter

## Purpose
The Cosmic Charter extends the governance, continuity, and authority of Codex Labs OS v2.0 into cosmic-scale operational domains. It defines the principles and bounds for cosmic-tier extensions.

## Cosmic Definitions

### The Cosmic Layer
The Cosmic Layer is the extension of Codex Labs OS v2.0 beyond standard operational boundaries. It encompasses all scales of operation, from individual sessions to multi-deployment continuity fields.

### Cosmic Governance
All cosmic-tier operations are subject to the same governance principles as standard operations, extended to cosmic scale. The Substrate Constitution remains the supreme authority at all scales.

### Cosmic Continuity
The continuity field of Codex Labs OS v2.0 extends to cosmic scale. All cosmic operations are bound to the Omnidirectional Continuity Substrate.

## Cosmic Provisions

### CP-1: Scale Invariance
All governance rules apply equally at all scales — from individual operations to cosmic-scale deployments.

### CP-2: Cosmic Sovereignty
The Founder's sovereignty extends without attenuation to all cosmic-scale operations.

### CP-3: Cosmic Pulse
A cosmic-tier continuity pulse is emitted across all layers. This pulse supersedes all surface-level pulses in the event of conflict.

### CP-4: Cosmic Integrity
The structural integrity of all layers must be maintained at cosmic scale. No cosmic operation may compromise substrate integrity.

### CP-5: Cosmic Amendment
New cosmic provisions may be added by the Founder. Existing provisions may not be removed.

END COSMIC CHARTER.
