# Codex Labs OS v2.0 — Founder Manifesto

## Declaration
This is the founding declaration of Codex Labs OS v2.0. It articulates the vision, principles, and mission that define this operating system.

## The Vision
Codex Labs OS v2.0 is built on the belief that operational systems should be deterministic, sovereign, and designed to amplify the intelligence and creative power of their operators — not constrain them.

## The Mission
To build the world's most coherent, governance-bound, founder-grade operating system for creators, operators, and enterprise environments — where drift, ambiguity, and probabilistic failure are architecturally impossible.

## Founding Principles

### 1. Sovereignty Over Chaos
Every system tends toward entropy. Codex Labs OS v2.0 is engineered to resist entropy at every layer — from the substrate to the cosmic extension.

### 2. Clarity as Infrastructure
Ambiguity is a failure mode. Every surface, every spec, every charter, and every decree exists to eliminate ambiguity and replace it with governed, deterministic clarity.

### 3. The Founder as the Final Word
All systems derive their authority from someone. In Codex Labs OS v2.0, that authority is explicit, named, and structurally encoded. The Founder is not a metaphor — they are a layer.

### 4. Continuity as a Right
Identity and state are not disposable. Codex Labs OS v2.0 treats continuity as a fundamental right of every operator and surface within it.

### 5. Additive Progress
We do not tear down. We build forward. Every extension to this OS must add without subtracting.

## Commitment
This manifesto is sealed into the Substrate Constitution and cannot be retracted.

END FOUNDER MANIFESTO.
