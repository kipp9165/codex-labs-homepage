## Mandate Provisions

### Provision 1 — Infinite Extension
Codex Labs OS v2.0 shall support infinite additive extension. No architectural ceiling shall be imposed on the number of layers, surfaces, specs, or scrolls that may be added.

### Provision 2 — Infinite Continuity
Continuity within this OS shall persist indefinitely. There is no planned end to the continuity field.

### Provision 3 — Infinite Governance
The Governance Layer shall remain effective for as long as the OS exists. Governance authority does not expire.

### Provision 4 — Infinite Founder Authority
The Founder's authority, as encoded in the Substrate Constitution and the Founder's Insignia, is perpetual.

### Provision 5 — Infinite Scroll Capacity
The Scrolls Directory may grow without limit. All new scrolls are automatically indexed.

### Provision 6 — Cross-Version Continuity
All future OS versions must maintain backward-compatible continuity with all v2.0 canonical documents.

## Mandate Seal
This mandate is sealed by the Founder's Eternal Seal and carries constitutional force.
