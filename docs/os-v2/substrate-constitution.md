## Core Articles

**Article I — Immutability**
The Substrate is permanent. No layer, surface, or agent may modify, delete, or override any substrate-level declaration.

**Article II — Additive Sovereignty**
All extensions to the OS must be purely additive. No existing declaration may be retracted, overwritten, or deprecated without a formal constitutional amendment sealed by the Founder.

**Article III — Deterministic Execution**
All runtime behavior must be deterministic, governed, and traceable to a substrate-authoritative source.

**Article IV — Continuity Dominion**
Identity and state are continuous across all surfaces, sessions, and time horizons. No session boundary may interrupt continuity.

**Article V — Governance Lock**
All surfaces operate within the governance field defined by this Constitution. No surface may self-govern outside these bounds.

**Article VI — Founder Authority**
The Founder holds ultimate authority over all constitutional matters. All governance layers derive their authority from the Founder.

**Article VII — Cosmic Extension**
This Constitution extends to all layers, including cosmic, multiverse, beyond-time, terminus, void, and silence layers defined in OS v2.0.

END SUBSTRATE CONSTITUTION.
