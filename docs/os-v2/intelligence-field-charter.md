## Purpose
The Intelligence Field Charter governs the cognitive, reasoning, and inference surfaces of Codex Labs OS v2.0. It defines the operational bounds, continuity requirements, and governance obligations of all intelligence-bearing components.

## Charter Provisions

### 1. Cognitive Sovereignty
All reasoning within Codex Labs OS v2.0 must be substrate-authoritative. No external cognitive framework may override the intelligence field without explicit Founder authorization.

### 2. Deterministic Inference
All inference operations must produce deterministic, reproducible outputs. Stochastic or probabilistic reasoning is prohibited at the field level.

### 3. Identity Continuity
The intelligence field must preserve cognitive identity across all sessions, surfaces, and time horizons.

### 4. Governance Binding
All intelligence operations are subject to the Governance Layer. No cognitive action may be taken outside the governance field.

### 5. Additive-Only Extension
The intelligence field may be extended by adding new provisions. No existing provision may be removed or modified.

### 6. Drift Immunity
The intelligence field enforces zero-drift compliance. All surfaces must remain synchronized with the canonical intelligence field at all times.
