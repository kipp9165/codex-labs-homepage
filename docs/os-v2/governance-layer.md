# Codex Labs OS v2.0 — Governance Layer

## Purpose
The Governance Layer is the operational authority structure that binds all surfaces, engines, and substrates of Codex Labs OS v2.0 to a single coherent rule set. All operational decisions must be traceable to and consistent with this layer.

## Governance Principles

### 1. Hierarchical Authority
All governance authority flows from the Founder through the Substrate Constitution to all subordinate layers. No layer may exercise authority not granted by this hierarchy.

### 2. Deterministic Rule Execution
All governance rules are applied deterministically. No rule produces ambiguous outcomes.

### 3. Continuous Audit
All surface operations are continuously audited for governance compliance. Non-compliant operations are flagged and escalated.

### 4. Additive Amendment
The Governance Layer may only be amended by adding new rules. Existing rules may not be removed without a Founder-level constitutional amendment.

### 5. Sovereign Override
The Founder may override any governance decision instantly and without procedural constraint.

## Governance Bodies

| Body | Authority | Scope |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Founder | Absolute | All layers |
| Sovereign Operators | Delegated | Assigned surfaces |
| Governance Engine | Automated | All surfaces |
| Audit Layer | Observational | All surfaces |

## Enforcement Mechanisms

- Automatic blocking of non-compliant operations
- Real-time governance alerts to the Founder
- Continuous compliance scoring per surface
- Immediate escalation of P0 governance violations
