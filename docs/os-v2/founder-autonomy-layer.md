## Founder Rights

### 1. Unconditional Override
The Founder may override any governance decision, routing rule, entitlement restriction, or operational policy at any time without justification.

### 2. Constitutional Amendment
The Founder alone may amend the Substrate Constitution. All amendments are effective immediately upon declaration.

### 3. Layer Creation
The Founder may create new layers, surfaces, charters, specs, decrees, maps, and scrolls at will. All new layers are automatically sovereign.

### 4. Layer Dissolution
The Founder may dissolve any non-constitutional layer. Dissolution is final and immediate.

### 5. Entitlement Grant
The Founder may grant any entitlement to any party, including entitlements not previously defined in the Entitlements Layer.

### 6. Eternal Continuity
The Founder's identity, authority, and vision persist across all sessions, surfaces, and temporal boundaries without interruption.
