# Whale-Tier Service Level Agreement (v2.0)

## Section 1 — Purpose
This SLA defines operational guarantees for Whale-Tier Licensees.

## Section 2 — Availability
Codex Labs OS v2.0 guarantees:
- 99.99% sovereign continuity uptime
- deterministic routing across all surfaces
- mission-critical admissibility response within 50ms

## Section 2A — Measurement Scope
"Mission-critical admissibility response" is measured as platform response latency
from Codex Labs edge ingress to first admissibility decision event under
contracted Whale-Tier production load profiles and agreed monitoring regions.

## Section 2B — Uptime Measurement and Exclusions
- The 99.99% continuity uptime target is measured per calendar month.
- Uptime excludes pre-announced scheduled maintenance windows.
- Uptime excludes force majeure and third-party network/provider outages outside
  Codex Labs operational control.

## Section 3 — Support
Whale-Tier receives:
- founder-grade support
- priority routing diagnostics
- continuity substrate verification

## Section 4 — Incident Response
Severity 1: 15 minutes  
Severity 2: 1 hour  
Severity 3: 4 hours  

## Section 5 — Continuity Guarantees
- omnidirectional continuity  
- substrate alignment  
- deterministic execution  

## Section 5A — Guarantee Definitions
- "omnidirectional continuity" means continuity telemetry remains active across
  all contracted Whale-Tier surfaces during service operation windows.
- "substrate alignment" means routing and policy configuration remains in sync
  with the approved Whale-Tier governance baseline.
- "deterministic execution" means identical admissibility inputs produce
  identical decision outcomes under equivalent runtime state.

END SLA.
