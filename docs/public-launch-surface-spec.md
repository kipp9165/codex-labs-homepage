# Codex Labs OS — Public Launch Surface Specification (Segment 1)

## Purpose
The Public Launch Surface defines how Codex Labs OS presents itself to external viewers. It ensures that the homepage inherits substrate guarantees, binds to continuity, and reflects the true architecture of the OS. It replaces placeholder content with deterministic, governed structure.

## Core Principles
1. The homepage must bind directly to the Substrate and Surface Protocols.
2. No marketing surface may override deterministic OS structure.
3. All public-facing content must propagate continuity tokens.
4. The homepage must present the OS architecture clearly and consistently.
5. The launch surface may extend metadata but never override substrate authority.

## Required Homepage Sections
1. **OS Identity Block**  
   A deterministic declaration of Codex Labs OS identity.

2. **Structural Overview Block**  
   A concise representation of the seven OS layers.

3. **Governance Block**  
   A summary of deterministic guarantees and governance rules.

4. **Scroll Access Block**  
   A governed entry point into public scrolls.

5. **Continuity Block**  
   A statement of identity persistence and deterministic execution.

## Deterministic Launch Guarantees
- No probabilistic homepage rendering.
- No stochastic interpretation layers.
- All homepage states must resolve to a single authoritative output.
- All homepage metadata must bind to continuity and identity tokens.
- All homepage logs must be compatible with Diagnostics Layer introspection.

## Additive-Only Rules
- Homepage extensions must be metadata-only.
- No dynamic execution at the launch surface.
- No external homepage surfaces may attach without Substrate approval.
