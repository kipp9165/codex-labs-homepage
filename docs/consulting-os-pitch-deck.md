## Slide 2: The Problem

**Founders and operators face three critical gaps:**

1. **Lack of clarity.** No clear decision architecture. Decisions feel reactive, not strategic.
2. **No direction.** Goals exist, but pathways are foggy. Constraints aren't mapped. Bottlenecks aren't surfaced.
3. **Structural drift.** Systems work until they don't. Ownership is unclear. Handoffs fail. Momentum gets lost.

**Result:** Chaos. Wasted effort. Burnt teams.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution

**Consulting OS delivers structural clarity through async, written-only analysis:**

- **Clarity Map** — Visual diagnosis of current state, constraints, and bottlenecks.
- **Direction Statement** — Clear, single-page articulation of intent and pathways.
- **30-Day Plan** — Specific, sequenced actions to move from current to desired state.
- **6-Month Plan** — Strategic roadmap with milestones and continuity checkpoints.
- **Risk Map** — Explicit failure modes, dependencies, and recovery protocols.
- **Momentum Protocol** — Weekly execution cadence and continuity checkpoints.

---

## Slide 4: The Process

**Async-first, no meetings.**

1. **Intake** (self-service form) — Share current state, desired state, constraints, timeline.
2. **Review** (24-48 hours) — Deep read and structural diagnosis.
3. **Session** (written consultation) — Questions, recommendations, and direction.
4. **Deliverables** (structured output) — Maps, statements, plans, and protocols in clean formats.
5. **Integration** (your team) — Implement, refine, and execute.

**Timeline:** 7 days to 30 days depending on engagement depth.
