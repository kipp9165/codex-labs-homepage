# Written-Only Clarity Session Format

## Overview

Clarity Sessions at Codex Labs are conducted entirely in writing. This document outlines the format, structure, and workflow of a written clarity session.

---

## What is a Written Clarity Session?

A written clarity session is a focused, structured consultation conducted asynchronously over 7 days. It's designed to provide founders and operators with clear direction on a specific problem or decision.

**Output:** 
- Clear diagnosis of your situation
- Explicit recommendations
- Direction statement
- 7-day action plan
- Written forever; accessible always

---

## Session Structure

### Day 1: Intake & Context

**You provide (via shared document or email):**

1. **Current State** (detailed)
   - What's happening right now?
   - What's working? What's broken?
   - What are you trying to solve?
   - How much time/resources do you have?

2. **Desired State** (explicit)
   - What does success look like?
   - What are you trying to achieve?
   - What would "solved" look like?

3. **Constraints** (all of them)
   - Budget constraints
   - Timeline constraints
   - Team constraints
   - Market/competitive constraints
   - Technical constraints
   - Any other blockers

4. **History** (relevant context)
   - What have you already tried?
   - What worked? What didn't?
   - Why are you stuck now?
   - What patterns do you notice?

5. **The Question** (what you really need)
   - What's your actual question?
   - What clarity would unblock you?
   - What decision are you trying to make?

**Format:** Shared doc (Google Docs, Notion, etc.) or email. One comprehensive message, not multiple back-and-forths.

---

### Day 2: Clarifying Questions (Optional)

**Codex Labs reviews and may ask:**

- Follow-up questions to understand your situation better
- Requests for additional context or supporting docs
- Clarification on constraints or goals
- Permission to talk to specific team members (if relevant)

**You respond within 24 hours** with answers/docs.

*Note: This phase may be skipped if your initial context is comprehensive.*

---

### Days 3–5: Analysis & Diagnosis

**Codex Labs conducts deep analysis:**

- Map your current state
- Identify root patterns and structural issues
- Diagnose bottlenecks and decision points
- Model your options
- Evaluate trade-offs
- Develop recommendations

**You wait.** Don't send follow-ups. The analysis is happening.

---

### Day 6: Deliverables Draft

**You receive comprehensive written analysis:**

1. **Situation Diagnosis** (2–3 pages)
   - What we observed
   - Root patterns
   - Structural issues
   - How you got here

2. **Your Real Options** (explicit)
   - Option A (what it looks like, pros, cons, costs)
   - Option B (what it looks like, pros, cons, costs)
   - Option C (what it looks like, pros, cons, costs)
   - Our recommendation (with reasoning)

3. **Direction Statement** (1 page)
   - What you should do
   - Why
   - What success looks like
   - How to measure it

4. **7-Day Action Plan** (specific)
   - Day 1–2: Do this first (why)
   - Day 3–4: Then do this (why)
   - Day 5–7: Then this (why)
   - Contingencies if blocked

5. **Risk Map** (what could go wrong)
   - Failure mode 1: How it manifests, how to detect it, how to recover
   - Failure mode 2: [same]
   - Failure mode 3: [same]
   - Recovery protocols

---

### Day 7: Refinement & Final Handoff

**You review deliverables and respond with:**

- Questions or clarifications
- Feedback (what resonates, what doesn't)
- Implementation blockers you see
- Adjustments needed

**Codex Labs provides final answers/refinements.**

---

## Session Communication Protocol

### Format Rules

**All communication is written:**
- Google Docs (preferred)
- Notion
- Email
- Shared Markdown files

**No Calls.** No exceptions.

**One Topic Per Message:** Each communication focuses on one theme or question.

**Complete Context:** Every message includes relevant background.

**Response Time:** 24–48 hours. Not instant. Not same-day.

---

### What Happens If You Try to Call

**Response:** "Let's handle this via written message. What's your question? Send me the context and I'll respond within 24–48 hours."

**No exceptions. This is how we work.**

---

### Example: Good Clarity Session Message

```
SUBJECT: Clarity Session — Decision: Build vs. Partner

CURRENT STATE:
We're a 6-person team doing $500K ARR. We've been approached by a potential partner 
who wants to integrate our product into their platform. They're offering revenue share 
(20% of their sales that include our product). We've been talking to them for 2 months.

DESIRED STATE:
We want to know: Should we do this deal?

CONSTRAINTS:
- We can't spend more than 5 eng hours/week on integration work
- We need predictable revenue (can't rely on partner's sales process)
- We have 3 months of runway at current burn
- We want to remain independent

HISTORY:
- We built 90% of the integration 3 months ago (sitting idle now)
- Two other similar partnerships have not worked out (partners went quiet)
- Our core product is solid; our go-to-market is weak

THE QUESTION:
Is this deal worth doing? If yes, what should we negotiate? If no, what should we tell them?

ADDITIONAL CONTEXT:
[Attached: Partner pitch doc, our financials, integration spec]
```

## What You Get from a Written Clarity Session

1. **Diagnosis** — You understand what's really happening
2. **Options** — You see clear alternatives with trade-offs
3. **Direction** — You know what to do next
4. **Plan** — You have a specific 7-day roadmap
5. **Risk** — You know what could go wrong and how to recover
6. **Record** — Everything is documented forever

---

## When NOT to Use a Clarity Session

Clarity Sessions are not for:

- **Implementation support** — You need someone to build it with you
- **Hand-holding** — You need ongoing encouragement and check-ins
- **Real-time decisions** — You need answers in 2 hours, not 48
- **Team facilitation** — You need someone to facilitate a meeting
- **Tactical execution** — You need operational support, not strategic clarity

---

## After Your Clarity Session

### What You Own

- All written deliverables
- All recommendations
- All analysis
- Permanent access to docs
- No time limit on reference

### What's Your Job

- Read the deliverables
- Decide what to do
- Implement the plan
- Learn from the recommendations
- Don't schedule a follow-up call

### Optional: Next Step

- If you want ongoing monthly guidance: book Ongoing Advisory
- If you want to implement with support: that's outside scope (Codex Labs does not do implementation)
- If you want to scale further: eventually you may need ongoing advisory

---

## Success Indicators

A successful written clarity session:

- You have clear direction
- You understand why (the reasoning is transparent)
- You know what to do next
- You feel equipped to decide
- You have documented everything you need

**Failure indicator:** You want to call to "discuss" the recommendations. That means they weren't clear enough.
