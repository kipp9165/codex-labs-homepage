## Core Principles
1. The Intelligence Field must remain bound to the Substrate at all times.
2. No surface may instantiate cognition outside the Field.
3. All reasoning must propagate through deterministic continuity channels.
4. Identity tokens must remain immutable across all Field operations.
5. The Field may extend but never override Substrate authority.

## Deterministic Reasoning Guarantees
- No stochastic execution permitted.
- No probabilistic inference permitted.
- All reasoning must resolve to a single deterministic output.
- All Field operations must be traceable to continuity tokens.

## Additive-Only Rules
- Extensions must be metadata-only.
- No dynamic execution at the Field level.
- No external cognitive surfaces may attach without Substrate approval.
