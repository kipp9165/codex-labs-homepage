Deterministic Continuity Guarantees

  • Continuity activation preserves one coherent operating lineage from launch through execution.
  • No continuation can bypass missing receipts, revoked state, or unbound conditions.
  • Equivalent launch conditions produce the same continuity admission outcome across governed surfaces.
  • Continuity drift is observable because inherited state is structurally compared before admission.
