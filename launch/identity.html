Launch Identity Activation Surface

A governed surface for activating launch identity with deterministic issuance, bounded protocols, and immutable evidence.

Identity

The launch identity surface establishes the named launch actor, authority scope, issuance state, and activation lineage required before any launch action is admissible.

Activation Protocols

Authority Binding

Bind the launch identity to a versioned authority object and a declared operating scope.

State Admission

Admit activation only when identity state, launch conditions, and receipt lineage are structurally aligned.

Receipt Finalization

Finalize identity activation with immutable launch receipts that preserve provenance across surfaces.

Deterministic Identity Guarantees

  • Identity activation resolves to a single admissible authority state.
  • Launch identity cannot be activated from ungoverned or ambiguous inputs.
  • Every activation path produces the same receipt class for the same declared conditions.
  • Identity drift is detectable because issuance lineage is preserved across every governed handoff.

Immutable Launch Tokens

Identity Token

Locks the issuing entity, authority version, and activation timestamp into a non-rewritable token boundary.

Condition Token

Captures the exact admissibility conditions under which identity activation was accepted.

Receipt Token

Preserves launch evidence so downstream systems can verify the same identity activation result without reinterpretation.

Multi-Surface Identity Activation Matrix

Operator Surface

Activates declared launch identity, validates authority scope, and produces the initial activation receipt.

Governance Surface

Confirms admissibility, enforces activation constraints, and verifies that identity issuance satisfies policy state.

Runtime Surface

Consumes immutable identity tokens and executes only against the admitted launch identity.

Evidence Surface

Stores receipt lineage so identity activation can be audited, replayed, and compared across launch events.
