Launch Governance Activation Surface

A governed surface for activating launch governance with deterministic constraint enforcement, versioned authority, and immutable evidence.

Identity

The governance surface identifies the authority model, constraint registry, and active policy state that determine whether launch execution is admissible.

Activation Protocols

Authority Resolution

Resolve the active authority object, governing version, and constraint scope before any governance state is activated.

Constraint Admission

Admit governance activation only when launch conditions satisfy declared constraints and invariants without override.

Disposition Sealing

Seal the governance outcome into immutable receipts so downstream execution inherits the same admissibility decision.

Deterministic Governance Guarantees

  • Governance activation always resolves against a single versioned authority state.
  • Constraint outcomes are reproducible because the same declared conditions produce the same admissibility decision.
  • Unauthorized launch paths cannot become admissible through runtime reinterpretation.
  • Governance divergence is detectable because every disposition is anchored to immutable governing evidence.

Immutable Governance Tokens

Authority Token

Locks the active governing authority, policy version, and control scope into a stable activation boundary.

Constraint Token

Captures the exact invariants and admissibility conditions that governed the launch decision.

Disposition Token

Preserves the accepted or rejected governance outcome so every surface can verify the same control state.

Multi-Surface Governance Activation Matrix

Operator Surface

Declares the requested launch action and submits it into governed authority and constraint review.

Governance Surface

Evaluates policy version, invariants, and consequences before sealing a deterministic disposition.

Runtime Surface

Accepts only the sealed governance disposition and blocks any execution that falls outside admitted control state.

Evidence Surface

Retains governance receipts and token lineage so launch authority can be audited without ambiguity.
