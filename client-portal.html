Client Portal

Governed client surface — deterministic access protocols, immutable client records.

Client Portal Identity

Surface: Codex Labs Client Portal Surface v2.0

Operator: Kipp M. Wiggins — Founder, Codex Labs

The Codex Labs Client Portal is the governed access layer for active clients. All access, deliverable distribution, and client communications are managed through deterministic protocols with full continuity and immutable record-keeping.

Governance

  • All client access is authorized by the Founder and governed by the Client Portal access protocols.
  • Access credentials are unique to each client and non-transferable.
  • Portal access is granted upon confirmed engagement activation and revoked upon engagement close.
  • All portal actions are logged and immutable once committed.
  • Governance decisions regarding client access supersede all other considerations.

Deterministic Access Protocols

  • Protocol CP-01: Access is provisioned only after engagement confirmation and payment where applicable.
  • Protocol CP-02: Each client receives a unique access record with documented permissions and scope.
  • Protocol CP-03: Deliverables are published to the client record upon Founder authorization.
  • Protocol CP-04: Client communications are logged within the client record — no off-record commitments.
  • Protocol CP-05: Access revocation requires a documented governance action with notification to the client.
  • Protocol CP-06: Portal disputes are escalated to the Founder and resolved within the governance framework.

Continuity

The Client Portal Surface maintains full continuity across all active and archived client records. No client record is deleted or altered without a documented governance action. All prior deliverables remain accessible to clients within their access scope.

Immutable Client Record Structure

  • Client ID: Unique identifier assigned at engagement activation.
  • Engagement Type: Category and scope of active engagement.
  • Activation Timestamp: UTC timestamp of engagement activation.
  • Deliverables Log: Ordered list of all deliverables with timestamps and authorization records.
  • Communications Log: Immutable record of all governed client communications.
  • Access Record: History of all access grants, modifications, and revocations.
  • Close Record: Final disposition on engagement close — outcomes, sign-off, and archive status.

Accessing Your Portal

Active clients with provisioned access should use their assigned credentials to access portal resources. If you are an active client and require access assistance, contact the Founder via the Contact surface with your Client ID.
