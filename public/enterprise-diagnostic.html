Enterprise Clarity Diagnostic (ECD)

A founder-grade, enterprise-scale diagnostic for organizations of 500+ employees.

What ECD Provides

The ECD identifies structural misalignments across clarity, governance, architecture, execution, and vendor strategy.

  • Leadership & architecture intake
  • Structural scan across 5 axes
  • Red/Amber/Green scoring
  • ECD Summary (2–3 pages)
  • ECD Map (1 page)
  • ECD Recommendations (1–2 pages)

Diagnostic Axes

  • Clarity Axis — decision rights & ownership
  • Architecture Axis — system coherence
  • Governance Axis — escalation & predictability
  • Execution Axis — stability vs. firefighting
  • Vendor Axis — alignment to operating model
