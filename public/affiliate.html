Affiliate FAQ

Do I need a Stripe account?

No. You do not need a Stripe account, a connected bank, or any technical setup to be an affiliate.

How do I get my unique link?

Email to request your affiliate ID, then use your link with ?ref=YOUR_ID attached, like ?ref=jason.

How do I get paid?

Payouts are manual. When commissions are due, payment is sent directly through Venmo, PayPal, Cash App, Zelle, bank transfer, or check based on your preference.

What happens when someone buys through my link?

The referral code is stored, the purchase is manually reconciled, and the commission is paid directly after attribution is confirmed.

Can I promote this offline (flyers, word of mouth)?

Yes. Offline promotion is welcome. You can share your referral link in print, text, email, or word of mouth so long as people can reach the page with your unique ?ref=YOUR_ID code.
