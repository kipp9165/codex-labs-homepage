Consulting OS — How We Work

Every consulting engagement with Codex Labs follows a structured clarity and direction system. You receive concrete deliverables, not vague recommendations.

  • Clarity Map — Diagnostic visualization of your current state, constraints, and bottlenecks.
  • Direction Statement — Clear, single-page articulation of intent and execution pathways.
  • 30-Day Plan — Specific, sequenced actions to move from current to desired state.
  • 6-Month Plan — Strategic roadmap with milestones and continuity checkpoints.
  • Risk Map — Explicit failure modes, dependencies, and recovery protocols.
  • Momentum Protocol — Weekly execution cadence and structural continuity checkpoints.

Sessions are available for founders, operators, and teams navigating complexity, scaling challenges, or strategic shifts.
