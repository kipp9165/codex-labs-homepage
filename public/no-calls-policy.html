Operating Model

No Calls Needed

Codex Labs operates asynchronously. No calls. No meetings. No real-time requirements.

Everything is written. Every decision is documented. Every recommendation is traceable. This is how we work: async-first, written-only, and structurally focused.

Why No Calls?

  • Calls are expensive. They interrupt flow, require context-switching, and produce no written record.
  • Async is clear. Written communication forces precision. No ambiguity. No "I thought you said."
  • Async scales. You can engage Codex Labs on your timeline, not ours. No scheduling friction.
  • Async is traceable. Every recommendation, every decision, every question lives in a written record.
  • Async respects autonomy. You respond when you're ready. You implement at your pace. You control your time.

How Written-Only Works

Structured, deliberate communication replaces real-time dialogue.

  • Context First. Every message includes background, constraints, and known friction points.
  • One Question at a Time. Don't ask multiple questions. One clear question. One focused response.
  • 48-Hour Response Cadence. You send a message; you get a response within 24â€“48 hours.
  • No Back-and-Forth. Communication is structured to minimize rounds. Clarity on first exchange.
  • Documented Decisions. Every recommendation lives in shared docs. Accessible. Reviewable. Implementable.

Communication Channels

Codex Labs uses a single channel per engagement: email or shared document (Google Docs, Notion, etc.).

  • No Slack. No Teams. No chat apps.
  • No back-and-forth via email threads. Structured messages, clean formatting.
  • No video calls. No Zoom. No screen sharing.
  • All communication is text-only, deliberate, and documented.

For Founders and Operators

Codex Labs is built for founders who value clarity, structure, and autonomy. If you need calls, meetings, and real-time support, we are not the right fit.

  • You are comfortable writing. You can articulate your situation in text.
