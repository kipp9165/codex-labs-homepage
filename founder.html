About the Founder

The architect behind Codex Labs.

Kipp M. Wiggins is the founder and architect behind Codex Labs — a digital product lab built for clarity, momentum, and modern creators. His work focuses on designing systems that help people think sharply, operate intentionally, and move with purpose.

Codex Labs is the culmination of years of studying operational structure, decision-making, and the psychology of execution. It is a place where systems thinking meets practical tools for founders, operators, and ambitious individuals.
