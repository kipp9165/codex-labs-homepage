Finalization Engine

Governed, deterministic finalization for every consequential state transition.

Identity

The Finalization Engine is a sovereign subsystem of Codex Labs OS. It holds a persistent, unforgeable identity bound to the governance manifest at the moment of instantiation. No finalization action may be attributed to an anonymous or unregistered surface.

Governance

All finalization decisions are subject to the governing conditions encoded in the governance manifest. The Finalization Engine does not hold discretionary authority; it executes only within the bounds of approved finalization rules and emits a governance receipt for every disposition it applies.

Deterministic Finalization Protocols

Each finalization event follows a deterministic protocol: authority validation → state boundary check → admissibility evaluation → disposition application → consequence capability issuance → evidence emission. The protocol is non-branching and non-optional. There is no secondary finalization path.

Immutable Finalization Tokens

Upon successful finalization, the engine issues an immutable finalization token scoped to the specific state transition, surface, and timestamp. The token is unforgeable, freshness-bound, and recorded to the append-only evidence plane. A token that does not appear in the evidence plane is not a valid finalization.

Multi-Surface Finalization Matrix

The Finalization Engine governs all consequential surfaces — execution, delivery, billing, identity, and audit — through a unified finalization matrix. Each surface row in the matrix carries its own admissibility rules, disposition options, and token namespace. The matrix is externally attestable and cannot be modified at runtime.
