Seal Engine

Governed, deterministic sealing across every consequential surface and state boundary.

Identity

The Seal Engine is a sovereign subsystem of Codex Labs OS. It carries a persistent, unforgeable identity registered in the identity manifest at instantiation. No seal may be issued or attributed to an anonymous, unregistered, or expired surface identity.

Governance

Seal authority is scoped and non-discretionary. The Seal Engine operates exclusively within the governing conditions established by the governance manifest. Every seal disposition triggers a governance receipt, providing an externally verifiable record of which rule authorized the seal event.

Deterministic Seal Protocols

Each seal event executes a deterministic sequence: identity binding → authority verification → state readiness check → seal admissibility evaluation → seal disposition → immutable token issuance → evidence emission. The sequence is non-bypassable. There is no alternative seal path and no discretionary override.

Immutable Seal Tokens

A completed seal produces an immutable seal token scoped to the surface, state snapshot, and timestamp at the moment of sealing. The token is cryptographically bound, freshness-limited, and written exclusively to the append-only evidence plane. Any seal not represented by a token in the evidence plane is not a valid seal.

Multi-Surface Seal Matrix

The Seal Engine manages sealing across all governed surfaces — execution, delivery, billing, identity, and audit — through a unified seal matrix. Each surface entry defines its own readiness criteria, admissibility conditions, token namespace, and evidence linkage. The seal matrix is externally attestable and locked against runtime modification.
