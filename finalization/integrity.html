Integrity Engine

Governed, deterministic integrity enforcement across every surface, state, and evidence boundary.

Identity

The Integrity Engine is a sovereign subsystem of Codex Labs OS. Its identity is persistently registered in the identity manifest and cannot be reassigned, forked, or anonymized at runtime. All integrity assertions are attributed to this registered identity and are externally verifiable.

Governance

Integrity governance is non-discretionary and manifest-bound. The Integrity Engine validates state transitions against the governing conditions and emits a governance receipt for each integrity assertion. Assertions that fall outside approved governing conditions are refused and recorded as structural violations in the evidence plane.

Deterministic Integrity Protocols

Every integrity check follows a deterministic protocol: identity resolution → governing condition evaluation → state consistency verification → violation detection → disposition → integrity token issuance → evidence emission. The protocol admits no branching, no silent pass-through, and no runtime override. An integrity check that does not emit a token and an evidence record did not occur.

Immutable Integrity Tokens

Each passed integrity check produces an immutable integrity token scoped to the surface, state hash, and check timestamp. Tokens are freshness-bound and written exclusively to the append-only evidence plane. Tokens cannot be revoked, amended, or back-dated. A surface state lacking a corresponding integrity token in the evidence plane has not been integrity-verified.

Multi-Surface Integrity Matrix

The Integrity Engine applies integrity enforcement across all governed surfaces — execution, delivery, billing, identity, and audit — through a unified integrity matrix. Each surface row specifies its consistency rules, violation thresholds, disposition options, token namespace, and evidence linkage. The integrity matrix is append-only, externally attestable, and cannot be modified at runtime.
