Intake

Governed intake surface — deterministic protocol, immutable record structure.

Intake Identity

Surface: Codex Labs Intake Surface v2.0

Operator: Kipp M. Wiggins — Founder, Codex Labs

The Codex Labs Intake Surface is the governed entry point for all new client, partner, and operator relationships. Every intake submission is processed against a deterministic protocol and stored as an immutable record.

Governance

  • All intake submissions are reviewed by the Founder within the committed response window.
  • Intake data is collected only as required for engagement scoping — no excess data collection.
  • Intake records are immutable once committed — no retroactive modification without a documented governance action.
  • All parties are notified of their intake record status within the committed timeline.

Deterministic Intake Protocol

  • Step 1 — Submission: Prospective client or partner submits intake form with full context: name, organization, engagement type, and stated objective.
  • Step 2 — Acknowledgment: Codex Labs acknowledges receipt within 24 hours and assigns an intake record ID.
  • Step 3 — Review: Founder reviews submission against current capacity, fit criteria, and governance constraints.
  • Step 4 — Response: A governed response is issued within the committed window: accept, defer, or decline — with documented rationale.
  • Step 5 — Record Commit: All intake actions are committed to the immutable intake log with timestamp and outcome.

Continuity

The Intake Surface maintains continuity across all submissions and outcomes. Prior intake records remain accessible and valid. No intake history is deleted or invalidated without a formal governance action.

Immutable Record Structure

  • Record ID: Unique identifier assigned at submission acknowledgment.
  • Timestamp: UTC timestamp of submission and each subsequent action.
  • Submitter Identity: Name and organization as provided at submission.
  • Engagement Type: Category of requested engagement.
  • Stated Objective: Verbatim objective as submitted.
  • Outcome: Final disposition — accepted, deferred, or declined — with rationale.
  • Operator Signature: Founder authorization stamp on all committed records.

Submit an Intake Request

To initiate an intake submission, contact the Founder directly via the Contact surface. Include your name, organization, engagement type, and stated objective. Your submission will be processed against the Deterministic Intake Protocol above.
