Codex Commission

Commission a sovereign instrument built specifically for your context — no templates, no shortcuts.

Codex Labs accepts a limited number of commissions each quarter. A commission is a custom-built intelligence instrument — a tool, workflow, or system — designed from first principles for your specific use case.

What You Can Commission

  • Custom intelligence dashboards and reporting tools
  • Sovereign workflow automation systems
  • Procedural briefs and decision frameworks
  • Operator-grade data pipelines and exports
  • Bespoke integrations with Codex Labs products
  • Research instruments and analysis modules

How It Works

  1. Submit the intake form — describe your context, goals, and constraints.
  2. We review your brief — within 3–5 business days.
  3. Scope & quote — we confirm scope, timeline, and pricing.
  4. Payment & build — work begins after payment is confirmed.
  5. Delivery & handoff — sovereign artifact delivered with full documentation.

Pricing

Commission pricing is scoped per project. Most commissions fall between $500 and $5,000 depending on complexity and timeline. Enterprise and ongoing retainer arrangements are available.
