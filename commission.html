Codex Commission

Codex Labs takes a small number of custom commissions each quarter. If you need a bespoke intelligence system, dashboard, or product built on the Codex stack — fill out the intake form below and our team will follow up within 2 business days.

What We Build

  • Custom intelligence dashboards and operator surfaces
  • Bespoke detection and analysis pipelines
  • Branded Codex Mikey Copilot integrations
  • Internal tooling and workflow automation
  • White-label GuessVision or DeerSafe deployments
  • Enterprise data export and integration layers

How It Works

  1. Submit the intake form — describe your project and goals.
  2. Discovery call — we'll schedule a brief call to scope the work.
  3. Proposal & agreement — we'll send a fixed-scope proposal for review.
  4. Build & deliver — we execute on the Codex stack and deliver your system.
