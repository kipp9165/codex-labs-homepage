Governed Resilience Engine

A deterministic resilience layer that governs recovery, containment, and continuity under stress across all Codex Labs surfaces.

Identity

  • The Resilience Engine is the bounded recovery layer for governed operating surfaces.
  • It defines how the system absorbs disruption without losing constitutional coherence.
  • It ensures resilience is structural, not improvisational.

Governance

  • Resilience actions execute under pre-declared authority and do not depend on ad hoc interpretation.
  • Every fallback path is versioned, reviewable, and reversible under one governance chain.
  • Incident containment cannot override identity, lineage, or audit continuity.

Deterministic Resilience Protocols

  • Failure states map to explicit containment, recovery, and return-to-service paths.
  • Fallback sequences preserve minimum viable operation until the primary state is restored.
  • Retry, rollback, and replay behaviors resolve from fixed rules rather than operator improvisation.

Immutable Resilience Tokens

  • resilience-engine-v1 — canonical engine identity token.
  • resilience-fallback-lock — immutable token for approved fallback execution paths.
  • resilience-return-seal — immutable token for governed restoration to primary state.

Multi-Surface Resilience Matrix

  • OS Surface: preserves authority continuity, rule survivability, and deterministic recovery logic.
  • Runtime Surface: preserves route availability, asset fallback behavior, and release survivability.
  • Founder Surface: preserves decision continuity, communication clarity, and operator confidence under pressure.
  • Commerce Surface: preserves product access, referral continuity, and transaction recovery posture.
