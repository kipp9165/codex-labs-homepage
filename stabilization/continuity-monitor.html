Governed Continuity Monitor

A deterministic monitoring layer that verifies continuity, lineage, and survivable operation across every governed Codex Labs surface.

Identity

  • The Continuity Monitor is the standing observer for uninterrupted operational lineage.
  • It verifies that governed surfaces remain connected to the same canonical operating chain.
  • It protects continuity from fragmentation, abandonment, and silent surface divergence.

Monitoring Protocols

Deterministic Continuity Guarantees
