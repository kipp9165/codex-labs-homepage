DeerSafe

A Codex Labs vertical product for safety, clarity, and environmental awareness.

What DeerSafe Is

DeerSafe is a Codex Labs micro‑system designed to increase situational awareness and reduce risk in wooded, rural, or low‑visibility environments.

Core Components

  • Environmental scanning protocol
  • Movement safety heuristics
  • Visibility and distance assessment
  • Risk‑reduction behaviors

Who It's For

DeerSafe is designed for individuals operating in rural or wooded areas where wildlife, terrain, or visibility can introduce risk.

How It Fits Into Codex OS

DeerSafe is a vertical product — a focused, standalone module that plugs into the broader Codex OS ecosystem.
