DeerSafe

AI‑Powered Wildlife Detection & Risk Intelligence.

Real-time anomaly detection for drivers, operators, and safety systems. Built for clarity, speed, and sovereign control.

What DeerSafe Does

DeerSafe is a visual intelligence instrument that detects wildlife, anomalies, and collision risks in real time. It is engineered for drivers, fleets, insurers, and operators who need reliable, high-speed detection.

  • Real-time wildlife detection
  • Collision-risk scoring
  • Anomaly classification
  • Heatmap and event exports
  • Telemetry overlays
  • Operator-grade performance

Who It's For

  • Drivers — Detect wildlife and hazards before they become collisions.
  • Fleets — Reduce incidents, improve safety, and protect vehicles at scale.
  • Insurers — Analyze wildlife risk, validate events, and reduce claims.
  • Researchers — Study wildlife patterns and environmental signals with precision.

How It Works

  1. Capture video or images — from dashcams or mobile devices.
  2. DeerSafe analyzes the feed — detecting wildlife, anomalies, and risk.
  3. Receive structured outputs — events, heatmaps, summaries, and telemetry.
  4. Export or integrate — for fleets, insurers, or research workflows.

Features

  • Wildlife detection
  • Risk scoring
  • Heatmap exports
  • Event summaries
  • Telemetry overlays
  • Enterprise scroll integrations
  • Metered billing support
  • Operator dashboards
