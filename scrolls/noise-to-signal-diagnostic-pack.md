# Noise to Signal Diagnostic Pack

This pack identifies where operational noise overwhelms useful signal. It provides a direct method for finding confusion sources and restoring clarity. Use it when teams are busy but outcomes are inconsistent.

## Symptom Capture
- Collect recurring complaints and delays.
- Identify where decisions stall or repeat.
- Separate one-off incidents from chronic patterns.

## Signal Criteria
- Define what counts as actionable signal.
- Set quality thresholds for incoming inputs.
- Remove vanity metrics from decision flows.

## Noise Source Mapping
- Trace noise to channels, meetings, or tools.
- Quantify interruption frequency by team.
- Mark high-noise interfaces for redesign.

## Diagnostic Interviews
- Ask operators where context is lost.
- Capture handoff failures with concrete examples.
- Validate observations across multiple roles.

## Correction Plan
- Cut redundant channels and reports.
- Tighten decision inputs to required fields.
- Publish new intake and escalation rules.

## Stabilization Review
- Re-measure latency and rework after changes.
- Confirm operators can identify real priorities.
- Lock improvements into recurring rituals.
