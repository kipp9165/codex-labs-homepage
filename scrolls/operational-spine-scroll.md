# Operational Spine Scroll

The operational spine is the minimum structure that keeps execution coherent under load. It defines ownership, rhythm, and escalation so work does not fragment. Use this scroll to stabilize teams moving through rapid change.

## Spine Definition
- Define the core operating lane from intake to delivery.
- Mark each handoff point explicitly.
- Remove optional steps from the critical path.

## Role Anchors
- Assign one accountable owner per stage.
- Clarify support roles versus decision roles.
- Publish ownership in one shared record.

## Weekly Rhythm
- Set one planning ritual and one review ritual.
- Cap agenda scope to operational blockers.
- Preserve ritual timing even during fire drills.

## Escalation Ladder
- Define trigger conditions for escalation.
- Route escalations to specific decision owners.
- Close escalations with documented resolution.

## Work Visibility
- Track commitments, status, and blockers in one board.
- Use common status definitions across teams.
- Prevent hidden work from entering delivery lanes.

## Integrity Checks
- Run periodic audits on handoff quality.
- Measure rework caused by unclear ownership.
- Refactor the spine when bottlenecks repeat.
