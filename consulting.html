Consulting OS

Governed consulting protocols for founders and operators.

Consulting Identity

Surface: Codex Labs Consulting OS v2.0

Operator: Kipp M. Wiggins — Founder, Codex Labs

Codex Labs Consulting OS delivers structured, deterministic advisory engagements for founders, operators, and organizations seeking sovereign operating infrastructure. Every engagement is governed, logged, and delivered against documented protocols.

Governance

  • All consulting engagements are authorized by the Founder before activation.
  • Scope, deliverables, and outcomes are defined in writing prior to engagement start.
  • No consulting output is released without Founder review and sign-off.
  • All engagement records are immutable once committed to the Consulting OS log.
  • Governance decisions override scope creep at every layer.

Consulting Protocols

  • Protocol C-01: Every engagement begins with a documented intake session defining scope, constraints, and success criteria.
  • Protocol C-02: Deliverables are specified deterministically — no vague outcomes accepted.
  • Protocol C-03: All recommendations are grounded in the Codex Labs OS operating principles.
  • Protocol C-04: Client records are maintained with full continuity across engagement phases.
  • Protocol C-05: Engagement close requires formal sign-off from both parties and a logged completion record.

Continuity

The Consulting OS maintains unbroken continuity across all active and prior engagements. All prior commitments remain in force. Continuity is enforced at the surface layer — no engagement history is discarded or invalidated without formal governance action.

Deterministic Guarantees

  • Scope is fixed at intake — no unilateral scope changes mid-engagement.
  • Deliverables are binary: delivered or not delivered — no partial credit.
  • Timelines are committed and logged; delays are governed by documented change protocols.
  • All pricing and engagement terms are disclosed upfront with no hidden conditions.

Engagement Types

  • OS Audit: Full review of your operating system, decision architecture, and execution infrastructure.
  • Systems Build: Co-design and deployment of sovereign operating infrastructure for your organization.
  • Founder Advisory: Ongoing strategic advisory for founders navigating scale, clarity, and operational leverage.
  • Protocol Design: Custom deterministic protocols for teams, products, and operating layers.
