GuessVision

AI-Powered Viral Prediction & Entertainment Intelligence

Guess the future of culture. Predict what goes viral. A real-time entertainment intelligence instrument for creators, analysts, and operators.

What GuessVision Does

GuessVision analyzes cultural signals, formats, trends, and audience behavior to forecast what will resonate — and why. It transforms entertainment intuition into measurable intelligence.

  • Viral prediction scoring
  • Format analysis
  • Audience resonance mapping
  • Trend trajectory modeling
  • Content opportunity detection
  • Competitive signal comparison

Who It's For

  • Creators — Predict what formats will hit. Understand what audiences want next.
  • Analysts — Track cultural signals and forecast entertainment trajectories.
  • Studios — Evaluate concepts before greenlighting. Reduce risk with intelligence.
  • Operators — Use real-time cultural data to guide decisions and strategy.

How It Works

  1. Submit a concept — a format, idea, or entertainment premise.
  2. GuessVision analyzes cultural signals — trends, formats, audience behavior.
  3. Receive a prediction report — resonance, virality, trajectory, and opportunity.
  4. Iterate with Codex Mikey Copilot — refine, reposition, or evolve the concept.

Features

  • Viral scoring
  • Format clustering
  • Audience resonance modeling
  • Trend forecasting
  • Opportunity mapping
  • Competitive signal analysis
  • Procedural briefs
  • Exportable reports
  • Integration with Codex Mikey Copilot
