Codex Labs Affiliate Handbook

A practical guide for affiliate partners.

The Codex Labs Affiliate Handbook is a simple guide for people who want to earn by sharing Codex Labs products.

Core Terms

  • High-percentage commissions on qualifying sales.
  • Zero cost to join.
  • No inventory, no physical logistics.

How to Share

  • Use your unique affiliate link.
  • Share on platforms where you already have trust.
  • Focus on clarity: explain what Codex Labs is and who it’s for.

Best Practices

  • Be honest and transparent.
  • Avoid hype; focus on value.
  • Share your own experience with the products when possible.

Affiliates are partners in the Codex Labs ecosystem. The goal is long-term, sustainable, mutual benefit.
